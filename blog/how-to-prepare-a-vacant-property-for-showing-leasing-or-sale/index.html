How to Prepare a Vacant Property for Showing, Leasing, or Sale

This guide is written for Bay Area office managers, property managers, landlords, investors, and preservation/vendor contacts who need practical cleaning decisions rather than generic advice.

Clear access and decide what stays

Confirm authority to enter the property, provide access instructions, and mark items that must remain. Remove personal documents and valuables before a crew arrives. Share photos of debris and abandoned belongings so the estimate reflects their volume.

Put the jobs in the right order

Complete dusty repairs before the final cleaning when practical. Confirm water and electricity are available. Arrange removal of large items before detail cleaning, and leave time between the final visit and the showing to inspect the rooms.

Walk the property like a visitor

Check the entrance, kitchen, bathrooms, visible floors, and odors. Open only the cabinets and appliances included in the cleaning scope. Make a short punch list with photos and discuss any remaining issues before the property is handed over.
