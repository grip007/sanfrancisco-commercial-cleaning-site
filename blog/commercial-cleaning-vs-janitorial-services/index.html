Commercial Cleaning vs. Janitorial Services: What’s the Difference?

This guide is written for Bay Area office managers, property managers, landlords, investors, and preservation/vendor contacts who need practical cleaning decisions rather than generic advice.

The terms overlap; the task list matters

Janitorial service usually describes repeat visits for everyday upkeep. Commercial cleaning is a broader label that can include those visits and occasional detail projects. Providers use these terms differently, so compare the actual proposal rather than the service name.

What to compare in two estimates

Check the rooms covered, visit frequency, floor methods, restroom tasks, waste handling, supplies, and access arrangements. Ask whether periodic work such as appliance interiors or detail cleaning is included or charged separately.

Choose the arrangement your building needs

A recurring plan helps maintain an occupied building. A one-time clean can address a move, a change in tenant, or accumulated dirt. If you need both, ask for distinct scopes so you can understand what each price covers.
