How Often Should an Office Be Professionally Cleaned?

This guide is written for Bay Area office managers, property managers, landlords, investors, and preservation/vendor contacts who need practical cleaning decisions rather than generic advice.

Start with use, not just square footage

A lightly used office may need fewer visits than a smaller space with frequent customers. Count the people using the space, the days it is occupied, and the restrooms and shared kitchen areas. These are starting points for a walkthrough, not fixed cleaning requirements.

Example schedules to discuss

For a lightly occupied suite, discuss one or two visits each week and a daily in-house check of bins and spills. A regularly occupied office may need several visits a week. A busy customer-facing office may need attention each business day. Adjust the plan when bins, restrooms, or floors need service before the next visit.

Separate daily tasks from detail work

Waste, restrooms, and food areas often need attention more frequently than interior glass or baseboards. Put the recurring tasks and periodic detail tasks on separate lines in the proposal. Review the results after the first few weeks and change the frequency where needed.
